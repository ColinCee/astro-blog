    Part 3: How Jev performs on Aliens

    holding shoot

    82%

    win rate, the dumb baseline

    jev

    80%

    win rate, a general model

    Jev is a quick classifier by TypeSafe. I wanted to see it in action after seeing it play Doom, turns out it's actually pretty decent for a generalized model, though not close to models trained specifically on the game.

    What's Jev?

    each pass, and what it outputs

    {"move": "left"}
    left
    42%
    right
    36%
    shoot
    22%

    An LLM runs once for every token it writes, so even a short structured answer like {"move": "left"} takes about six passes, and it gives you the move, not a probability for every choice. Jev runs once and gives every choice a probability, in about 200 ms.

    costEach Jev call is about 200 ms, so a full game of 2,000 moves takes around 7 minutes.
