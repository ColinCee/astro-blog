Grey does the structure. The accent points.

The one reference for how the site looks. Every comp and every build reads its colours from tokens.mts and its rules from this page; when a decision changes, this page changes in the same commit. What was tried and dropped lives in docs/design-rejected.md.

Principles

What every decision comes back to.

  1. One thing per glance. The name leads; one thing moves on the first screen; one accent, used sparingly.
  2. Real over decorative. Real numbers, live weather, real posts and roles. A slogan or a placeholder figure gets cut.
  3. Personality in the content, not the chrome. The one-liners, 張志權 and the homelab carry the character; surfaces stay grey and flat.
  4. Light type. Thin for the voice, semibold only for names. Anything that reads thick gets lighter.
  5. The terminal shows, it does not ask. Real-looking commands with real output; nothing to type; nothing that repeats the page.
  6. Each fact once. If it is in the hero, the CV or a post, it does not appear again.
  7. Motion has a cause. Slow, pauses on hover, off with reduced motion.
  8. Fewer options. Once a choice is made the comp loses the switch, and the dropped idea goes in the rejected list.

Colour

Three kinds of colour and nothing else: one grey scale, one accent scale, three status colours. Each step is one OKLCH value; only lightness changes between light and dark, so dark mode is the same palette, not a second one.

neutral · hue 255, barely tinted cool · 12 steps

accent · one hue · 12 steps

status · green ok, amber warn, red danger · steps 3 (wash), 9 (dot), 11 (text) only

surfaces

page = n3 · panel = white (dark: n3 on n1) · well = n4

separated by tone, never by a shadow

text

ink = n12 · for titles and values

muted = n11 · the line under a title

faint = n10 · dates, labels, captions (4.5:1)

edges

line = ink at 8% · strong = 14% · faint = 5%

dark: white at the same alphas

Shape and depth

Small, consistent corners and no shadows. Depth comes from tone: a white panel on a grey page, a darker well inside a panel.

8px

6px · controls
