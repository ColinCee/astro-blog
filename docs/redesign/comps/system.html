colincheung.dev · design system

Grey chrome, one accent, two dots.

What the polished sites have in common is not more colour. It is a designed grey scale, borders cut from the ink, small layered shadows, and type whose tracking tightens as it grows. Colour is rationed to one hue with two jobs. This page is every token, in both themes.

Scales

Each step is one OKLCH value: the hue and chroma are fixed, only lightness changes between light and dark. Step 9 (the solid) is identical in both themes.

Rules

  • Grey chrome. Surfaces, borders, icons and text are the neutral scale. If something is coloured, it is saying something.
  • The accent has two jobs: the new value of a result that got better, and links. Not buttons, not headings, not decoration.
  • Status is a dot. Green and amber appear as dots and tiny badges only, at steps 3/9/11.
  • Borders are cut from the ink (8% / 14%), never a solid grey hex.
  • Dark is the same scale at other lightnesses. No second palette; step 9 is identical in both.
  • Tracking tightens with size. −0.045em at the name, −0.01em in body.
  • One signature effect: the terminal. No gradients, glows or grain.
