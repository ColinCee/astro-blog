# Design System: colincheung.dev

> How the visual system is applied, so new pages stay on-brand. The visual
> reference, with every swatch, type sample and chart form drawn from the real
> tokens, is [`src/pages/design.astro`](src/pages/design.astro) (`/design/` on the
> dev server). Colours are generated by `docs/redesign/comps/tokens.mts` into
> [`src/styles/scales.css`](src/styles/scales.css); the roles are
> [`src/styles/tokens.css`](src/styles/tokens.css). Ideas tried and dropped are in
> [`docs/design-rejected.md`](docs/design-rejected.md).

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "Grey does the structure. The accent points."** The
eight principles behind it (one thing per glance, real over decorative, light
type, the terminal shows and does not ask, and the rest) are at the top of the
design page.

### Theme
Dark is the default; light is a soft toggle for daylight reading. The choice is
`data-theme="light"` on `<html>`, set before paint by an inline script in
`components/site/Head.astro` (from `localStorage`), and toggled by the button in
the nav, which also dispatches a `themechange` event so anything drawn in a canvas
can redraw. The system setting is not followed.

### Typography standards (enforced by `tests/typography.spec.ts`)
| Rule | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Body text (long-form) | 16-19px at every screen size | Below 16 strains; above ~19 reads as heavy. |
| Body line-height | 1.5-1.8 (currently 1.65) | WCAG 1.4.12 asks for at least 1.5. |
| Line length | 45-90 characters (currently ~88) | 45-75 is the classic ideal; WCAG 1.4.8 suggests 80. 88 is a deliberate choice for a wider column; do not go past 90. |
| Smallest text anywhere | 11px (`0.6875rem`) | Mono labels and metadata. Nothing smaller, including on phones. |
| Contrast | 4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text | WCAG 1.4.3 (not yet automated; `tokens.mts` checks the scales). |
| Units | rem for every size and width | So the large-screen scaling below applies to everything. |

The test measures the rendered page at phone, laptop, 2560 and 3840 widths and
fails if any rule is broken. Change a number here and in the test together.

### Large screens
The root font size scales gently with the viewport above ~1600px
(`clamp(100%, 0.85rem + 0.15vw, 110%)` on `html`): 16px on a laptop, about 17.4px
at 2560, capped at 17.6px. Every size and width is in rem, so type, columns and
spacing grow together. A stronger scale (20px at 2560) was tried and read as too
big.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Three kinds of colour and nothing else: one cool grey scale, one purple accent spectrum, three status colours.
- Dark and light are the same OKLCH scales at other lightnesses, not a second palette. No pure white or black surfaces.
- Sora for names and the voice, Geist for reading, DM Mono for data.
- Flat: surfaces separate by tone, never by a shadow or a border.
- Small, consistent corners: 8px panels, 6px controls, 4px chips; only dots are round.
- Controls are ink, never the accent.
- The literal terminal metaphor is scarce — reserved for the homepage.
- Widths are one three-tier scale: `--w-prose` (`46rem`, reading), `--w-page`
  (`68rem`, content pages) and `--w-poster` (`90rem`, the homepage). Content
  pages center nav, footer, and content on `--w-page` so their edges line up; the
  homepage centers its nav, terminal, and sections on `--w-poster`. Same system,
  wider tier, not an exception.

## 2. Colours

Each colour is one OKLCH value from `tokens.mts`; light and dark change only
lightness. Swatches are on the design page.

### Scales
- **Neutral** (`--n1`…`--n12`): hue 255, barely tinted cool, 12 steps.
- **Accent** (`--v1`…`--v6`): one purple spectrum at hue 308, 6 even steps from
  a dark purple up to the accent itself, the same in both themes. 308, not violet
  296 or blue 262: a hue loses colour as it lightens, and of the hues clear of the
  status colours, 308 keeps the most in both themes; violet read muted lavender
  in dark, and blue keeps less still and reads as a default link. It stops at the accent: the greys need
  a white end, the accent never does (pale tints read pink or white). Each theme
  picks its jobs from it (Roles below); why, against Radix, Tailwind, Material
  and others, is `docs/redesign/research/accent-ramp-research.md`. A four-hue
  palette (teal, ochre, indigo alongside the accent) read as busy.
- **Status** (`--ok`, `--warn`, `--danger`): green, amber, red, at steps 3 (wash),
  9 (dot) and 11 (text) only. There is no blue status colour; the accent stands
  for "info".

### Roles (`tokens.css`)
- **Surfaces:** `--bg` page (dark n1, light n5), `--panel` and `--pane` raised
  surfaces (dark n3; light n3 and n2), `--well` a darker tone inside a panel (n4).
- **Text:** `--ink` (n12) titles and values; `--muted` (n11) the line under a
  title; `--faint` (n10) dates, labels and captions, still 4.5:1;
  `--prose-ink` post body, a notch softer than ink in dark, ink in light.
- **Edges:** `--line` and `--line-strong`, cut from the ink at 10% and 16%, so
  every border shares its tint.
- **Accent:** each job is one spectrum step. Dark: `--accent` and
  `--accent-text` are v6, the accent itself (bars, lines, results, links);
  `--accent-solid` v4 for filled marks. Light and print step down one so they
  hold contrast on a light page: `--accent` v5, `--accent-text` v4 (4.5:1).
  `--accent-ink` (white) for text on a filled accent.
  Derived: `--accent-line` (40%, inline link borders) and `--ring` (20%, the state
  glow).
- **Charts:** `--bar` (n7) the "before" bar; `--gain` (ok11) and `--loss`
  (danger11) a real gain and a real drop.

### Print
The CV re-declares the tokens inside `@media print` to flip to ink-on-white for a
clean PDF: `--bg`/`--panel`/`--pane` become `#ffffff`, `--ink` `#111111`,
`--muted` `#333333`, `--faint` `#5a5a5a`, `--line` `#d5d5d5`, and the accents
become `#0e7490` (a print-safe teal). Site chrome (nav and footer) is hidden and
entries use `break-inside: avoid`. This is the only place the palette leaves the
two themes.

### Named Rules
**The Accent Points Rule.** The accent marks results and links, nothing else:
1. **The result**, the number or mark a page or figure exists to show: a post's
   headline value and a chart's result bar or line in full `--accent`; a result
   in small text (a terminal row, a table) in `--accent-text`. The old value is
   `--faint` before an arrow; a plain comparison like "82% vs 80%" stays ink.
2. **Links**, in `--accent-text` (`--accent-text` in prose).
3. **The one highlight in a hero drawing or one-liner.**
4. **A hovered control:** a button fills with `--accent-hover` (dark: v3 half
   over the panel; light: v5 at 16%) and its border goes `--accent-solid`, while
   its label or icon stays bright ink (a purple icon read dimmer than the white one at rest); a
   link's underline goes `--accent`. One at a time, so it
   never crowds a glance.
Chrome never takes it: prompts, carets, window dots, the nav, separators (the
worked-at slashes keep a 40% hint, `--accent-line`). Never a surface, a large
area, a heading or a button. If two things are purple in one glance, one is
wrong. A metric inside a sentence or a bullet is bold `--ink`. (Set 31 Oct 2026:
the accent on every prompt and slash made the homepage loud while the post results
read pale.)

**The Status Is Small Rule.** Green, amber and red appear only as dots and small
badges, always with a word, never colour alone. Red in the chrome means a real
failure (a homelab service down); a result that got worse stays ink. In charts,
`--gain` and `--loss` appear only on charts that compare runs, with a key.

## 3. Typography

**Names, headings, the voice:** Sora (`--head`), weights 200–600.
**Reading:** Geist (`--grotesk`), 400–600, with `ss01` on.
**Data:** DM Mono (`--mono`), 300–500.
**CJK:** `--cjk` stack (PingFang TC / Noto Sans TC/SC) for names like 張志權.

All three come from Google Fonts by a `<link>` in `components/site/Head.astro`,
with preconnects. Geist Mono was tried and clashed with Sora.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Sora 600, `clamp(2.6rem, 5.5vw, 4.25rem)`, −0.05em): the hero
  name. One per view, sentence case.
- **Headline** (Sora 600): post titles (`clamp(1.9rem, 5vw, 2.7rem)`, −0.045em),
  the reach-out line, section headings (`1–1.35rem`, −0.02 to −0.03em).
- **Voice** (Sora 200, punchline 400, −0.03em): the one-liners and the worked-at
  names. Heavier weights read as thick.
- **Title** (Geist 400, −0.01em): post and list titles. Never 500 or heavier in
  lists.
- **Body** (Geist 400, 16–17px, lh 1.55; posts 1.65): prose, ledes, the line
  under a title in `--muted`. Bold is 600.
- **Label** (DM Mono 300–500, `0.6875–0.8125rem`, no added tracking): the
  terminal, dates, stats, captions, kickers, tags, figure text.

### Named Rules
**The `//` Kicker System.** Sections are marked with a lowercase mono comment —
`// writing`, `// experience`, `// reach out` — in `--accent-text` (the blog index
and a post's back-link), `--muted` (the CV) or `--faint` (reach out). Keep it
lowercase, mono and comment-prefixed: never an all-caps tracked eyebrow, never
`01 / 02` numbered markers.

**Tracking Tightens With Size.** −0.05em on the name, −0.045em on post titles and
the reach line, −0.03em on headings and the one-liners, −0.01em on list titles,
none on mono. Letters never touch.

## 4. Elevation

Flat. Depth comes from tone, not shadows or borders: page (`--bg`), a raised
panel (`--panel` / `--pane`), a darker well inside it (`--well`). `--lift` and
`--wash` are `none`: no drop shadows, no glow behind the page, no gradients.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-By-Tone Rule.** No shadows on cards, panels, inputs or figures, and no
borders on panels. The one sanctioned glow is **`--ring`**
(`0 0 0 4px` accent at 20%), a response to state: the current or hovered node on
the blog index, the CV ledger and a post's timeline. Never ambient.

## 5. Components

### Navigation
- One `components/site/Nav.astro` on every page. A mono mark
  `colin@glasgow:~$` (ink, the `@` in the accent, `:~$` faint) on the left; links
  **Writing** and **CV** (Geist, `0.9375rem`, `--muted`) and the theme button on
  the right. (No About; contact lives on the homepage.)
- **Hover:** `--ink` with an accent `border-bottom`. **Active:** `--ink` with a
  `--line` border.
- **Theme button:** a control — ink icon (moon / sun), `--panel` fill, `--line`
  border, 6px corners; the border goes ink on hover.
- Centered on `--w-page`, so the mark lines up with the content's left edge and
  the links with its right edge (and with the footer). On the homepage it spans
  `--w-poster` and hides its links below 560px.

### Footer
- One `components/site/Footer.astro`: `Colin Cheung` (mono, `--muted`) and
  `© {year} · built in Glasgow` (`--faint`), centered on `--w-page`, `1px` top
  rule. On the homepage it spans `--w-poster`, its meta is `--muted`, and it ends
  with `last deploy <hash>`, linking the commit.

### Tags / Chips
- Mono, 11px, `--muted`, `1px solid var(--line)`, 4px corners,
  `0.18rem 0.62rem` padding, no fill. Used for the CV's stack lists.
- No hover state — they are quiet texture, not controls.

### Controls
- Ink on the panel tone (`--panel`, `--line` or `--line-strong` border), 6px
  corners: the theme button, the hero's GitHub and LinkedIn icons, the worked-at
  pause button. Never the accent, never a pill.

### Links (inline)
- `--accent-text` text with a `1px` **`--accent-line`** `border-bottom` that goes
  solid `--accent` on hover. Never underlined by default; the border is the
  affordance.

### Timeline (signature component)
- Blog index, CV ledger, and long-form article navigation share the **node**; the
  blog index and articles also share a vertical spine (`1px var(--line)`) with a
  **node** per entry: an 8–9px dot, `--bg` fill, `2px solid var(--faint)` ring.
  The **current / active / hovered** node switches its ring to `--accent` plus
  `--ring`. A mono date rail sits to the left of the spine; body content to the
  right.
- **Blog index (`pages/blog/index.astro`):** a `// writing` kicker and a Sora
  lede, then one row per post from its frontmatter: year, month and read time in
  the rail; the title in Geist 400, ink, an `--accent` underline on hover; under it the stat in
  mono (old value `--faint`, new one `--accent-text`, a `vs` comparison in
  ink) and its `statLabel` in `--faint`.
- **CV ledger (`pages/cv.astro`):** one `<details>` row per role, so the whole
  career fits one laptop screen closed. Closed, a row is the evidence: node, mono
  dates (`now` in `--accent-text` for the current role), company in Sora 600,
  title in mono with a `contract` chip (ink on `--well`, 4px) where it applies,
  and the headline result right-aligned in ink (six in a column would sprinkle the accent). Open, it unfolds in
  place (`::details-content` height and opacity, the page's one authored motion)
  into the context line, bullets and stack tags, indented to the company column.
  An `open all` / `close all` mono control sits on the section line, and every
  row opens for print. Hover tint is `--panel`, pointer devices only. Below 720px
  the row stacks dates, company, title and result with the chevron at the right.
  The `//` kickers and section heads on the CV are `--muted`; a metric inside a
  bullet is bold ink. Content is `data/cv.ts`; the pieces are `components/cv/`.
- **Article timeline (`layouts/Post.astro`):** the third use of the spine, and it is
  in the flow of the article, not a sidebar. When a post opens with three or more
  dated `##` headings (`16 December: they move the flight`), the layout splits the
  rendered Markdown at those headings and renders each section as a timeline entry.
  The date prefix is parsed off the heading (headings carry no year, so the last
  dated one is anchored to `pubDate` and the list is walked backwards) and the rest
  of the heading becomes the entry's title.
- At `64rem`+ the date sits in the left margin as a sticky column (large Sora day
  numeral, mono month and year, then the wait since the previous entry: `next day`,
  `+3 days`, `+8 weeks`, `+3 months`), with the spine and a node in the gap. Below
  that the spine runs down the left edge and the date sits inline above the title.
- A `// timeline` line above the list gives the first and last dates and the total
  span in days. Node state is the ring alone: unread `--faint`, passed filled
  `--accent`, current `--accent` plus `--ring`.
- Undated sections after the run (takeaways, reflections) render as ordinary prose
  below the timeline. Only use dated headings when the post really is a sequence.

### Homepage motion
- The name and the terminal rise in on load (the shared `rise` keyframe).
- One thing moves on the first screen: the reel. The terminal types its commands,
  then the writing lines print one after another.
- Worked at, Projects and Reach carry `data-reveal` and rise in on scroll (armed

- Reduced motion stops everything and shows each at rest (`Base.astro` zeroes

### Metric Highlight
- `.hl` — inline `<b>` in `--accent-text`, weight 600, `tabular-nums`, in post
  prose. Wraps the one number in a paragraph that is the point; any other number
  stays bold ink.

### Blog Post (long-form reading)
- Posts render on the shared `Base` shell via `layouts/Post.astro`; the prose styles are `styles/prose.css`. Below `64rem` it is a
  single `--w-prose` column. At `64rem` and up the post sits on `--w-page`, inset by
  `--rail` (`9rem`) plus `--rail-gap` (`3.5rem`), so timeline dates sit in the left
  margin and plain and timeline posts share one left edge. Content carries a `// writing` back-kicker, a Sora 600 title
  (`clamp(1.9rem, 5vw, 2.7rem)`, −0.045em), and a mono meta line (date · N min read).
- **Headline stat:** the post's `stat` renders under the title between hairlines,
  its `statLabel` above it in 11px mono `--faint`, the value in Sora 600. A result
  that got better shows the old value in `--faint`, an arrow, and the new one in
  `--accent`; a `vs` comparison stays ink.
- **Quotes** are excerpts on the raised surface (`--pane`, 8px, no border or rule),
  so quoted documents read differently from narration. **Ordered lists** are
  numbered takeaways: hairline-separated rows with mono `--accent-text` numerals.
- **Body size:** `--prose-size`, 16-17px (`clamp(1rem, 0.96rem + 0.18vw, 1.0625rem)`),
  line-height 1.65. Larger sizes read as heavy; do not raise it.
- **Figures** are components in `src/components/figures/`, used by name in a
  post's MDX (no import needed): give them the raw numbers and they compute the
  bars, positions and shares. Each one's styles sit beside it as a `.css` file,
  global and under `.prose`, all loaded for every post through `figures.css` in
  the order they apply. `<Fig title caption grow>` is the frame: one panel on
  `--pane`, 8px, no border, a short title above, a caption below; `grow` animates
  its bars in once when seen.
  Inside it: `<Cmp>` (before/after bars to scale; `from` makes a step of a
  waterfall, `ref` a dashed reference line, `cols` the column widths), `<Dots>` (a
  dot plot on one axis, in titled groups), `<Stack>` (one bar split into shares,
  with a legend), `<Split>` + `<Panel title>` (panels side by side),
  `<Morph>` (JSONL text rearranging into columns and back, built on `Frames`, text that moves between stages; on a loop while seen, hover pauses it, a tab stops it), `<Lookup>` (dictionary encoding: values pointing at numbers and the dictionary they look up, each entry's number in its own neutral style on both sides), `<Repeats>` (how Zstandard finds repeats on one column: before with the repeated stretch marked, after with it as one "repeat" chip), `<Boxes>` (a row of
  jobs or steps), `<Runs>` (blocks on a shared time axis: how long each run takes
  and how many fit), `<Splits>` (bars cut into parts with a key above; a part with
  no text is a sliver whose time goes after the bar; `est` rows are estimates,
  drawn lighter), `<Breakdown>` (one row per part of a total: explanation,
  bar, time and share) and `<Pts>` (win rate as the bar with the runs' range,
  win rate and points beside it; each change from `prev` in `--gain` green for a real gain,
  `--loss` red for a real drop, faint when the test can't tell it from luck, by the `real` flags; the kept
  row alone in the accent; `base` stripes the 2018 baseline; one dashed line
  for holding shoot; a key under the number columns names that line and says green / red is a real change and grey could be luck; there's no colour key, the rows label themselves: the striped row's tag says it's the baseline and the accent row's name is in the accent too (the accent is the post's subject: the final agent in part 2, Jev in part 3); column titles centred, linking the post's footnote on the test). `<Tally items>` is a few headline numbers in a row, each with a small label above, the first in the accent. `<Days then now thenLabel nowLabel>` draws time as one square a day, the `now` days lit in the accent. `<Json value fold>` draws a JSON request or reply like a code block, each string longer than `fold` characters cut to one line with an ellipsis that opens in full on a click. `<Decide tokens choices>` is a timeline (a Gantt chart) of an LLM against a decision model: a column per pass, left to right in time, each pass a bar with its output centred on top, a token for each of the LLM's passes and every choice's probability, stacked, for the decision model's one pass, its pick in the accent. Recordings are `<Clip src label rate hi>` (pixelated, playing only while
  on screen and never on their own under reduced motion); anything inside a Clip
  is its caption, and several sit side by side in a `<div class="vids">`. A post told as numbered steps can carry `<Recipe steps until>`: a
  panel floating in the right margin (only where the margin fits it), the passed
  steps in ink and the current one in the accent, shown from the "Step 1"
  heading until the `until` one.
  Marks are neutral (`--bar` / n7, estimates lighter, the baseline striped); only
  the result takes the accent. The chart palette and forms are drawn on the
  design page. Aim for at least one figure
  per post so no screen is text alone. Explanations sit next to what they explain
  (a short title above, labels on the marks), not in a paragraph under the figure.
  Every chart axis says what it measures and in what units. Figure text comes in two
  sizes only, at any screen width: 11px for titles, ticks and notes, 12.8px for labels
  on the marks and values. SVG charts get there through `--k` (set by the layout
  script), so their text never scales with the drawing.
- **Interactive figures** are components too, and load `src/scripts/widgets.ts`
  only on posts that use one: `<LineChart>` (a line chart from data: axes, an
  optional band, a line, dots and a legend; one function in
  `src/scripts/line-chart.ts` draws it at build time as the no-script fallback and
  in the browser at the figure's real width), `<Ladder>` (a step chart of a
  sequence of speed changes, x axis the change number; tap or drag across a step
  for its detail) and `<QView>` (a recording with the network's value for each
  move under it, a `.player` bar to pause and step one decision at a time, and a
  `.peek` block showing the four frames that decision saw, cut from a sprite
  sheet). Charts drawn in code run full width with text at true size.
- **Deep detail** goes in `<details class="more">`, linked from the sentence that
  needs it; the layout opens it on the jump.
- **Older figure types no post uses now**, written as HTML: `.ctl` (buttons and
  range inputs at least 44px tall), `.net` / `.slab` / `.deck` (a network's
  layers as tilted stacks of real activation maps), `.map` (a route over a game
  screen), `.gains` / `.gain` (before → after with a log-scale bar of the
  multiplier), `.loop` (a process diagram with a dot running each loop), `.arch` /
  `.cube` (a network as 3D blocks sized by each layer's shape),
  `.flow` (a game as a row of decision boxes shaded by score) and the `chain`,
  `reveal`, `pick` and `flow` widgets. Their styles are kept, marked, in
  `src/components/figures/`; turn one into a component when a post needs it.
- **Post header:** title, then the headline stat (when set), and the meta row: date
  and read time (prose words only, at 200 a minute; figures do not count). Header, prose, timeline and footer all share one measure, `--measure` (44rem),
  so every block on a post (text, figures, code, tables) has the same left and
  right edge. Wider "breakout" figures and code were tried and rejected (Oct 2026).
- **Reading progress:** a 2px accent bar fixed to the top of the viewport.
- **Post footer:** `older` / `newer` links by publish date, then `all writing`.
- **Sections:** each `##` (Sora 600) gets a hairline above it so long posts scan as sections.
- **`.prose`** styles the slotted Markdown: `--prose-ink`, `--prose-size`, line-height
  `1.65`, measure `--measure` (`44rem`). Headings are Sora 600 `--ink`; links are
  `--accent-text` with an `--accent-line` bottom border; list markers are
  `--accent-text`; bold is ink 600.
- **A wide screen grows the type, never the measure.** The measure is in rem, so it
  grows only with the root size on large monitors (see "Large screens"); the answer
  to a cramped big monitor is a filled margin, not a longer line.
- **Tables** are hairline data rails, not boxes: no outer border, a single
  `--line` rule under a mono `--faint` header row, and `--line` at 55% between
  rows. Column alignment in the Markdown drives the styling — a `---:`
  column renders mono, `tabular-nums` and `nowrap` so figures align on the decimal.
  A bold last row is a total, ruled off in ink.
  Below `40rem` the table scrolls horizontally in its own overflow container.
- **Code:** fenced blocks are rendered by Expressive Code (`github-dark` /
  `github-light`, switched by the site's `data-theme`, not the system setting) on
  `--pane` with `--line` borders and no shadow (set in `astro.config`); inline code
  is a mono chip on a faint `--ink` 10% wash, 4px corners. Never hand-restyle
  Expressive Code blocks.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep to the grey scale, the purple accent and the three status colours.
  Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large/bold ≥ 3:1 (`tokens.mts` checks the scales).
- **Do** give the accent only its three jobs: the improved value, links, small
  marks at the one thing. Leave the sentence around a number `--muted` or ink.
- **Do** mark sections with the lowercase `// kicker` system.
- **Do** keep type light: Sora 200 for the voice, 600 only for names and
  headings, Geist 400 for titles and reading, DM Mono for data.
- **Do** center nav, footer, and page content on `--w-page` so their edges line up;
  give long-form posts the narrower `--w-prose` reading measure.
- **Do** check every change in dark, light and at 375px.
- **Do** provide a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback for every animation; let
  hover pause anything that moves on its own.
- **Do** reach for the tokens instead of re-mixing or hardcoding: the surface steps
  (`--bg`, `--panel`, `--pane`, `--well`), `--line` / `--line-strong`,
  `--accent-line`, `--ring`, `--gain` / `--loss`, the corners (`--r`, `--r-ctl`,
  `--r-chip`), the motion scale (`--dur-fast`/`--dur-slow`, `--ease`/`--ease-out`)
  with the shared `rise` keyframe, and the width scale
  (`--w-prose`/`--w-page`/`--w-poster`).
- **Do** listen for `themechange` in anything drawn in a canvas.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use pure white or pure black surfaces; light is soft (a mid-grey page,
  off-white panels).
- **Don't** treat monospace as costume. Mono is earned technical chrome; the *full*
  terminal metaphor is homepage-only — don't cosplay a terminal on other pages.
- **Don't** add a second accent hue, use the accent for a surface, heading or
  button, or use gradient text or `background-clip: text`.
- **Don't** use colour alone for status; a dot or badge always has its word.
- **Don't** use cards as the default container, nested cards, or `border-left`/
  `border-right` coloured side-stripes on cards, list items, quotes, callouts, or
  alerts. Depth is tonal.
- **Don't** add drop shadows, glows or borders on panels. The only shadow is the
  `--ring` state glow.
- **Don't** over-round: 8px panels, 6px controls, 4px chips; only dots are round.
  No pills, no 12px+ corners.
- **Don't** ship the generic SaaS/Bootstrap/AI-blog-starter default look — if it
  reads as a template, it's wrong.
