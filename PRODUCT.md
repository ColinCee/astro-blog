# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primarily Colin Cheung's own space — a personal home + technical blog. Secondary
visitors:

- **Fellow engineers / developers** who land on a post (often from search or a
  shared link), skim for the insight, and decide whether to read and trust it.
- **Potential employers / collaborators** sizing up how Colin thinks and what he
  has shipped.

Context: read on both desktop and mobile, often skimming. They judge credibility
in seconds, then either read deeply or bounce.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that *feels like Colin* — somewhere to publish engineering
stories, discoveries, and opinions, and to present his identity as a senior
engineer with character. Not a corporate résumé and not a faceless blog starter.
Success = it reads as distinctive and personal, makes the writing inviting, and
signals craft and credibility without trying to "sell".

## Brand Personality

Confident, playful, engineer-native. Voice is punchy and plain-spoken with a bit
of dry wit; it lets real work and metrics do the talking rather than adjectives.
Emotionally it should feel alive, crafted, and a little fun — unmistakably a
builder's site, never a template.

Positive reference: **cocall.ai** — bold ALL-CAPS grotesk display type,
monospace technical accents, terminal/device "live" motifs, punchy declarative
copy, and a confident accent color on a high-contrast base.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / corporate landing-page templates.
- Dated / Bootstrap-y themes.
- Faceless, obviously AI-generated blog starters (the unmodified default look).
- The cream / beige "editorial-restraint" default body background.
- Monospace-as-costume: technical motifs must be earned and crafted, never
  decorative cosplay.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, not a résumé.** Let concrete metrics and real stories carry
   credibility instead of adjectives.
2. **Engineer-native, not corporate.** Terminal/technical motifs are welcome —
   but only executed with real craft, never as costume.
3. **Confident and playful beats safe.** Commit to a point of view; a little fun
   is the brand. Safe equals invisible.
4. **Reading is the product.** Posts must feel inviting and effortless to read at
   any screen size.
5. **Feels like Colin.** Personal, opinionated, distinctive — refuse template
   anonymity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast, large/bold text ≥ 3:1. Honour
`prefers-reduced-motion` with crossfade/instant fallbacks for every animation.
Keyboard-operable controls with visible focus states. Respect light/dark and the
user's system colour-scheme preference.
